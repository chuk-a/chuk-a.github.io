# AirQLander

AirQLander is both circuit and sculpture—a true Circuit Sculpture, inspired by the spectacular works of Mohit Bhoite, whose creativity and craftsmanship continually push boundaries. His creations revealed to me that electronics can be not only functional, but sculptural. This device doesn’t just measure air quality—it embodies it. It’s a fusion of engineering and art, where exposed geometry, sensor integration, and brass framing come together to express both data and design.
